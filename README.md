### 👋 About me

I'm a Flutter developer from Surat, India. I build production mobile apps — the kind that ship to the stores and get used every day, not weekend prototypes.

- 🏗️ **How I build** — feature-first clean architecture, Riverpod/BLoC for state, Isar and Firebase for data, offline-first where it matters
- 📱 **What I build** — cross-platform iOS and Android from a single codebase, dropping to native only where it earns its place
- 🩺 **Where I build it** — healthcare and wellness, education, marketplaces, and productivity products
- 🤖 **Currently exploring** — AI-assisted development workflows and on-device ML in mobile apps

> 🔒 Most of my work sits under client and product NDAs, so it lives in private repositories. The public repos here are a smaller sample — demos, take-home projects and experiments that show how I structure and reason about code rather than what I ship day to day. The contribution graph below tells the fuller story.
